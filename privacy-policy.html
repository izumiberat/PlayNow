Privacy Policy for Play Now (formerly PlayIdeas)

Last updated: 23/11/2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Play Now app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from its users. The app operates entirely offline after initial download.

2. Local Storage

The app stores your favorite activities locally on your device using secure storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or third parties.

The website play.izutech.fr only remembers your chosen language, your child's age group and the activities and small plans you save as favourites, in your own browser, so the page opens the same way next time. The app version of the website (play.izutech.fr/appli/) also keeps a copy of the activity list on your device so it works offline. Nothing is sent to us.

3. No Third-Party Services

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the app or on the website. If you choose to send an activity idea through the "Submit an idea" form, the details you type are sent to us by email through the form service Formspree, and are only used to review your idea.

4. Children's Privacy

While Play Now provides activity ideas for children, neither the app nor the website collects any information from children or requires any personal details.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions: contact@izutech.fr (Izumi Bérat, IzuTech, Pau, France)
